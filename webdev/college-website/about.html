About GCET Jammu

Learn about the history, academics and achievements of Government College of Engineering and Technology, Jammu.

Introduction

Government College of Engineering and Technology (GCET), Jammu was established by the Government of Jammu and Kashmir in 1993 with the approval of the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). The Institute was created with the vision of providing excellence in technical education and research. Its campus is spread over 869 kanals of land, approximately 22 km from Jammu city.

GCET Jammu is the first institution in the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir to have all five of its undergraduate programmes accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). The Institute was granted Autonomous Status by the UGC in 2024, effective from the academic session 2024-25, and is recognized under Sections 2(f) and 12(b) of the UGC Act, 1956.

The Institute offers B.Tech. programmes in Electronics & Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Mechanical Engineering and Civil Engineering. It also offers M.Tech. programmes in Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering and Mechanical Engineering, along with a lateral entry scheme for B.Tech.

GCET Jammu provides well-equipped laboratories and opportunities for technical education, research and innovation. The Institute also provides consultancy services to various government and semi-government organizations. Its Training & Placement Cell prepares students for professional opportunities and the changing requirements of the job market.

The Institute also conducts an Induction Programme for newly admitted students to develop creativity, confidence, leadership, time management and interpersonal skills. Over the years, GCET Jammu has continued to encourage innovation, entrepreneurship and practical solutions to engineering challenges.
